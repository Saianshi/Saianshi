### 🌾 Monsoon Twin
> 🥇 **Finalist at Avinya 2026, IIT Guwahati.** We were top 5 in Round 1 and made it to the 36 hour finale.

- A simulation of a 100 m² tomato polyhouse in Guwahati, running on real hourly weather data (ERA5)
- Compares three ways of controlling vents, fan and irrigation: a fixed timer, a simple threshold rule, and a predictive controller (MPC) that looks 12 hours ahead
- One interesting thing we found: during monsoon nights, opening the vents barely changes humidity because the outside air is already almost saturated. So we added a circulation fan as a second actuator
- The MPC controller brought leaf wet hours down from around 1453 to around 786 in the monsoon season compared to the timer
- Uses FAO-56, Tetens equations and plant disease models, and has 8 test gates to check the physics makes sense
- **Tech:** `Python` `NumPy` `Pandas` `Streamlit` `Plotly` `pytest`

🌐 **Live app:** https://guwahati-polyhouse.streamlit.app<br>
🔗 **Repo:** https://github.com/Saianshi/Monsoon-Twin

### ⚗️ Reactor Performance Prediction
> 🏅 **Finalist (Rank 14) at FUGACITY ML Hackathon, IIT Kharagpur.** Our final scores were RMSE 11.04, MAE 2.70 and R² 0.50.

- Predicting the yield of a plug flow reactor (A → B → C) with only 150 rows of training data
- Since the data was so small, we first fit the actual reaction kinetics (Arrhenius + ODEs) and then used a Gaussian Process to learn only the leftover error
- The physics model alone took the cross validation RMSE from 38.3 (just predicting the mean) down to 6.88
- We were careful with validation (10x10 K-fold and leave one out) and checked that the fitted parameters actually made sense
- **Tech:** `Python` `SciPy` `scikit-learn` `NumPy` `Matplotlib`

🔗 **Repo:** https://github.com/Saianshi/Reactor-Performance-Prediction

### 🚁 Disaster Drone Swarm
- Six drones take off from a 5G command van and fly to an earthquake damaged building
- One drone scans the building wall, and when its camera (OpenCV) spots the trapped person it sends an alert to the base station
- You can watch all 6 camera feeds and the drone positions live in RViz, and the whole thing starts with one launch file

### 🧭 Disaster Response Decision Engine
- Decides where to send rescue teams after an earthquake and flood when all communication is down and the only info comes from noisy drone sensors
- The engine never sees the real positions of victims, it only works with what the sensors report
- At first 97% of missions were failing. After finding why and fixing the search logic, mission success went up 5.6x
- The drone planner covers about 97% of the area, while a normal back and forth sweep gets stuck at about 32%
- Tested over 100 different random runs, and also made a dashboard for responders
- **Tech:** `Python` `NumPy` `Matplotlib`

🔗 **Repo:** https://github.com/Saianshi/Desaster-manegmenet-Drones
